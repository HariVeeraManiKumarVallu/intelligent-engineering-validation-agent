Engineering knowledge: simply supported beam with a central point load.

For a rectangular beam with width b, height h, span L, and central point load P:
Maximum bending moment M_max = P*L/4.
Maximum bending stress sigma_max = 6*M_max/(b*h^2).

Use SI units: P in newtons, L/b/h in metres, stress in pascals.
The allowable stress is a design constraint. A calculated stress at or below the allowable stress passes the stress constraint.
